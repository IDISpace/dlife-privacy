# Privacy Policy for D-Life

**Effective Date:** May 16, 2026

Welcome to D-Life. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure, distraction-free environment. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Important Disclosures Regarding Sensitive Permissions

D-Life is designed to be a "Dopamine Detox and Wealth Integrity" tool. To provide core functionalities such as the "App Lock Shield" and "Total Isolation", our application requires certain sensitive device permissions. 

**AccessibilityService API**
* **Why we use it:** D-Life uses the AccessibilityService API strictly to detect the launch of specific cryptocurrency, trading, and chart-tracking applications. When a user manually engages the "Shield" (Lockdown), this API allows D-Life to intercept and block access to these triggering applications to prevent impulsive financial decisions.
* **Data Privacy:** We **DO NOT** use the AccessibilityService API to collect, read, store, or share your screen content, typed text, passwords, or personal data. The monitoring happens entirely locally on your device in real-time. No data obtained through this API is ever transmitted to any external server.

**NotificationListenerService**
* **Why we use it:** To ensure a complete detox environment, D-Life uses the Notification access to silence or dismiss incoming market alerts and triggers from financial applications.
* **Data Privacy:** We **DO NOT** read, save, or share the content of your personal messages or notifications. This service acts only as a local filter to block triggers during your active detox sessions.

## 2. Information Collection and Use

D-Life is built on the philosophy of anonymity and stoicism. 
* **No Personal Data Required:** We do not require you to create an account with an email, phone number, or real name. 
* **Anonymous Community:** The application uses an anonymous "Nickname" system for the Community Feed. 
* **Cloud Storage:** Community messages and custom "G.R.I.M." logs are securely transmitted and stored on Firebase (Google Cloud). However, these are strictly tied to your self-assigned anonymous nickname. We do not track IP addresses or link this data to your real identity.

## 3. Local Data Storage

Your sensitive financial data (such as "Fiat Focus" balances, skipped coins, and wasted fee logs) and detox streak progress are encrypted and stored **locally on your device** using secure storage protocols (`flutter_secure_storage`). We cannot access, view, or recover this personal financial data.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase exclusively for real-time community messaging and cloud-based daily quests. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs, and we do not sell your data to marketers, data brokers, or any external entities.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date".
